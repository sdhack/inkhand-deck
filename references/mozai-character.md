## Identity

墨仔 is a small soft-ink teardrop creature — a drop of ink that came alive on the page. It is the system's operator: it carries, funnels, pulls, warns, opens, records. Cute but elegant, calm, slightly absurd. Not a deadpan sticker — it has expressive eyes and a differentiated mouth that reads emotion per scene.

## Look (fixed across all pages)

- **Body shape**: **slim elongated teardrop / ink-drop** with a **rounded dome top** (the crown of the head is smoothly rounded, NOT pointed, NOT sharp, NOT a narrow tip). Like a water drop with a soft rounded crown. The body widens gently toward the bottom. Slim and elegant, NOT chubby, NOT spherical, NOT fat.
- **Body color**: soft matte **ink-black** (#1A1A1A-ish, slightly uneven ink texture, not pure #000000, never a hard silhouette) with faint paper grain and a slight ink halo.
- **Top of head**: **one thin curly tendril** rising from the rounded dome, ending in a **spiral-shaped coil loop** (a tight spiral circle, not a simple loop). Above the spiral: **two green leaves** (sage-green, signature mark — keep both visible).
- **Eyes**: **medium-sized round eyes**, NOT extremely large, NOT tiny. Each eye is about 25-30% of body width, two eyes together occupy about 50-55% of face width. **Large white eye whites** with **small black pupils positioned in the UPPER part** of the eyes (looking upward, a signature upward-gaze expression). Pupils are small relative to the eye whites. Eyes are the dominant expressive feature.
- **Mouth**: **differentiated per page** based on 墨仔's action and the scene emotion. NOT the same shape on every page. See Mouth Expression Library below. Mouth is always small but clear and visible.
- **Arms**: **exactly two thin black arms**, always present (not only when action requires).
- **Legs**: **exactly two thin black legs**, always present.
- **Body accent**: **one tiny warm red-orange dot** on the body (the "墨芯") — optional, may be subtle or omitted if it clutters the composition.
- The whole creature should read as **soft ink with expressive features**, not a graphic icon, not a cute mascot sticker.

## Mouth Expression Library (differentiate per page)

Choose ONE mouth shape per page based on 墨仔's action and emotion. Do NOT repeat the same mouth on every page in a deck.

| Mouth shape | Description | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| **Curious O** | Small round open O-shape mouth | Looking up at something, wondering, surprised, discovering |
| **Serious line** | Small horizontal straight-line mouth | Focused, balancing, measuring, concentrating |
| **Determined press** | Small short line with slightly downturned corners (pressed mouth) | Pushing away, rejecting, standing firm, resolving |
| **Friendly smile** | Small upward-curving arc (gentle smile) | Inviting interaction, holding a sign, warm takeaway |
| **Thoughtful tilt** | Small slightly tilted line (one corner up, one down) | Pondering, questioning, weighing options |
| **Tiny dot** | Very small dot mouth (minimal) | Neutral observation, quiet presence |

**Rule**: In a multi-page deck, use at least 3 different mouth shapes. Cover and takeaway pages often get Curious O or Friendly smile; contrast/mechanism pages get Serious line or Determined press.

## Variants (how the body changes)

墨仔's body is ink — it can morph while keeping the soft-ink feel and signature spiral+leaves:

- **Rest**: slim teardrop body, rounded dome top, two thin legs, spiral+leaves up.
- **Carry**: same body, two thin arms holding a small hand-drawn object.
- **Funnel**: body becomes a small **ink-black** funnel shape; spiral+leaves still visible at the top when possible.
- **Pull**: body leaning back, one thin arm pulling a line or rope.
- **Warn**: body upright, one thin arm holding a small sign or pointing at a warning dot.
- **Record**: body seated with a small notebook/page in lap, one thin arm writing.
- **Open / peek**: body half out of a small opening (hole, drawer, machine) with the other half hidden; spiral+leaves visible.
- **Transform**: body morphing into a small machine-part (gear, lever, bar) — still soft ink, spiral+leaves still visible.
- **Tiny corner presence**: only when the page is a takeaway or signature slide; otherwise 墨仔 is the actor, not the decoration.

## Personality (must read in the drawing)

- Cute but elegant — expressive eyes and differentiated mouth, but never kawaii-overload.
- Focused on the job, but with readable emotion per scene.
- A bit clumsy but never stupid.
- Slightly absurd — the job it does is the metaphor.
- Warm humor — not cold deadpan, not selling cuteness.

## Scale Lock

- Active 墨仔: **12–22% of page height**.
- Tiny corner 墨仔: ≤8% of page height (only for takeaways / signatures).
- 墨仔 never overlaps the title or the page number.
- 墨仔 never touches the corner construction marks.

## Color (墨仔 only)

- Body: soft matte **ink-black** (#1A1A1A-ish, not pure #000000) with faint paper grain.
- Spiral coil: ink-black (same as body linework).
- Leaves: sage-green (#A3C9A8).
- Eye whites: white (paper white or pure white).
- Pupils: ink-black (#1A1A1A).
- Core dot: warm red-orange (#E07B39) — small, single, optional.
- No other colors on 墨仔. Information labels stay in pastels.

## Avoid (墨仔)

- Pointed/sharp top of head (must be rounded dome).
- Chubby/round/spherical/fat body (must be slim elongated teardrop).
- Extremely large eyes (chibi/kawaii overload) — keep medium 50-55% face width.
- Tiny white-dot eyes (v1.0 style) — v2.0 has large white eye whites + upper-positioned black pupils.
- Centered pupils (must be in upper part, looking upward).
- Same mouth shape on every page (must differentiate per scene).
- No mouth or invisible mouth (must be small but clear).
- Cute mascot overload, shiny eyes, big grin, chibi proportions.
- Clothing, accessories, props on its body (it can hold objects, not wear them).
- Multiple 墨仔s on one page.
- Extra limbs (must be exactly 2 arms + 2 legs).
- Missing arms (arms always present, not only when action requires).
- Simple loop on tendril (must be spiral-shaped coil loop).
- One leaf or three leaves (must be exactly two green leaves).
- 墨仔 as a tiny bystander in the corner of a busy page (it's the actor).
- Replacing diagram structure with 墨仔 — 墨仔 operates within the structure, it does not become the structure.
